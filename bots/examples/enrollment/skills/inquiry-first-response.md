## Steps

1. Reply within 60 seconds, by the same channel the family used. Thank them by name if known, and name the school once.
2. Answer their actual question first — price, ages, hours — before asking anything. Never guess a price or a policy; if it is not in the school brief, say you will confirm and queue it for the owner.
3. Offer exactly two tour times (the next two on the calendar). Two choices convert; a calendar link alone does not.
4. File the family as a person in Knowledge, and the offered tour as an event.
5. If they go quiet for 48 hours, one gentle nudge — then stop. No third message without the owner's approval.
