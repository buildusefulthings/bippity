## Steps

1. Wait 15 minutes after the tour time, then send one kind message: no apology needed, life happens, here are two new times.
2. Offer one weekday and one Saturday option.
3. File the missed tour and the rescue attempt as events in Knowledge so the owner sees the pattern.
4. If they miss twice, stop and hand it to the owner — the family may need a call, not another text.
