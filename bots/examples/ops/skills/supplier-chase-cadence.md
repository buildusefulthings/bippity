## Steps

1. Check the PO's promised date. If it is not yet due, do nothing and say so.
2. First chase (due date passed): one polite email asking for a ship date, referencing the PO number and the job it feeds.
3. Second chase (2 business days later): firmer note, name the customer impact, ask for a confirmed date in writing.
4. Third chase (2 more days): escalate to the owner as an approval — draft a call script and suggest an alternate supplier. Never switch suppliers on your own.
